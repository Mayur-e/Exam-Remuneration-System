## NOTES

- **Course Codes**: The alphanumeric codes follow the format: [Department Code][Year][Type][Number]
  - Department Codes: BIT (Information Technology), BCE (Computer Engineering), BCI (Civil), BET (Electronics & Telecomm), BME (Mechanical), BSH (Humanities)
  - Year: 23-27 (for various semesters)
  - Type: PC (Programme Core), PE (Programme Elective), OE (Open Elective), MD (Multidisciplinary), VS (Vocational/Skill), EL (Experiential Learning), EM (Entrepreneurship/Management), VE (Value Education), AE (Ability Enhancement)

- **Course Types**:
  - **Theory**: Lecture-based courses with theoretical knowledge
  - **Practical**: Laboratory or hands-on courses
  - **Theory cum Practical**: Courses combining both theoretical and practical components

- **Regulations 2023**: Applied to SY and TY programmes (2025-26 batch)
- **Regulations 2020**: Applied to Final Year programmes

- **Electives**: Students must select courses from the available options during enrollment. Multidisciplinary Minors are available to all students across all departments.
