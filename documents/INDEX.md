# Exam Remuneration System Documentation Index

Welcome to the official documentation for the **Exam Remuneration System**. This guide is divided into separate modules explaining each feature in detail, the roles involved, and how to use them.

## 📁 Feature Guides

1. **[Authentication & KYC](./01_Authentication_and_KYC.md)**
   *Roles: Faculty, External Examiners, Admin*
   - Registration, Google Login, Profile Bank Details, External KYC Uploads.

2. **[Internal Teaching Claims](./02_Internal_Teaching_Claims.md)**
   *Roles: Faculty, Coordinator, Admin*
   - Written assessment, Oral/Practical duty claims, and Automated calculations.

3. **[External Teaching Claims](./03_External_Teaching_Claims.md)**
   *Roles: Internal Coordinator, External Guest, Admin*
   - Magic links, Travel Allowances (TA/LCA), Guest KYC.

4. **[Non-Teaching Claims](./04_Non_Teaching_Claims.md)**
   *Roles: Faculty (Expert Assistant), Staff, Admin*
   - Roster management for Lab Assistants & Peons, Staff Norms validation.

5. **[Claim Review & Approval Workflow](./05_Claim_Review_and_Approval.md)**
   *Roles: Coordinator, Admin*
   - Technical screening, Rejections, and the "Send Back" correction cycle.

6. **[Disbursement & Accounts Management](./06_Disbursement_and_Accounts.md)**
   *Roles: Accountant (Billing In-charge)*
   - Excel Billing Sheet export, Bulk Payment processing, UTR tracking.

7. **[System Configuration & Rates](./07_System_Configuration_Rates.md)**
   *Roles: Admin*
   - Managing rate tables, Staff wage master, Academic year cloning.

8. **[Support & Ticketing](./08_Support_and_Ticketing.md)**
   *Roles: Faculty, Admin*
   - Query categories, threaded communication, and resolution tracking.

9. **[Dashboard & Reporting](./09_Dashboard_and_Reporting.md)**
   *Roles: All*
   - Analytics, KPI tracking, and PDF Remuneration Slip generation.
